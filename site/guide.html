Create a purchase requisition

Before you start

  • Your role can raise requisitions. If you can’t see New Requisition, ask your administrator.

Steps

    After you send it

    The requisition shows as Pending approval at the top of the list, and the approver is notified. Once it’s approved, Procurement can turn it into a purchase order.
