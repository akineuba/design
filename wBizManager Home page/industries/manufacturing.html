The problems we hear most often from manufacturing businesses before they move to wBizManager.

Materials run out mid-production

Raw material levels are checked by walking the store, so lines stop when a key input runs short.

Costs you cannot pin down

Material, labour and overheads are tracked in different places, so the true cost of each product is a guess.

Purchasing without sign-off

Urgent buys skip approvals, and the spend only shows up when the supplier invoice arrives.
