FAQs

Frequently asked questions.

Everything you need to know about wBizManager — from getting started to pricing, security and support.

Topics

General

What is wBizManager?

wBizManager is a cloud-based business management and ERP solution that helps businesses manage sales, accounting, inventory, purchasing, customer management, HR, expenses and reporting.

Who is wBizManager for?

wBizManager is designed primarily for SMEs and growing businesses that need a more structured way to manage core operations.

Is wBizManager cloud-based?

Yes. wBizManager is delivered as a cloud-based business management solution.

Is there a free trial?

Yes. A free trial option is currently offered, so you can explore wBizManager before choosing a plan.

How do I get started?

Start the free trial or get in touch. A member of the wBizManager team can also discuss your business requirements with you.

Which industries use wBizManager?

Teams in oil and gas services, energy and power, pharmaceuticals, distribution, manufacturing and professional services all use wBizManager. Each module can be configured around how your industry works.

Setup & onboarding

How long does it take to set up?

Most businesses are working in wBizManager within a few days. Setting up your organisation, users and opening balances takes an afternoon; larger rollouts with several entities or integrations are planned with our team.

Can I move my data from another system?

Yes. Customers, suppliers, items, opening balances and employees can be imported from CSV or Excel. Every import is checked row by row before anything is saved, so you can fix issues first.

Do you help with onboarding and training?

Yes. Every account gets guided setup and help articles, and our team can run training sessions for your staff. Full ERP plans include a dedicated account manager.

Can I choose which modules my team sees?

Yes. Roles and permissions control which modules, departments and actions each person can use, so people only see what they need for their work.

Pricing & billing

What counts as a full user?

A full user is anyone who signs in to work in wBizManager — raising invoices, approving requests or running reports. Every plan includes three full users, and you can add more when you configure your plan.

What is an employee record?

Employee records are the people your HR team manages. Records on the Full ERP and HR Suite plans include self-service, so employees can view payslips and request leave without needing a full user seat.

What is included in the free trial?

The free trial runs for 14 days with every business module, team member access and permissions, and custom approval workflows for one organisation. You do not need a credit card, and you can move to a paid plan at any point.

Can I change plans later?

Yes. You can move between plans or add organisations at any time, and we will adjust what you pay from your next billing date.

Can I run more than one organisation?

Yes. Each legal entity has its own plan and allowances. When you add another organisation it copies the first configuration, so you only change what is different.

Modules & features

Do the modules share data with each other?

Yes. Every module works from the same records, so an invoice in Sales updates Accounting, a purchase order updates Inventory, and timesheets flow into Project costs without re-entering anything.

Can I set up my own approval workflows?

Yes. Purchase orders, payments, leave and expenses can each follow their own approval steps, with conditions such as amount limits or departments, and every decision is recorded.

Does wBizManager support multiple currencies?

Yes. You can invoice, pay and post journals in other currencies, with exchange rates entered manually or updated daily, and gains or losses posted automatically.

Can I build my own reports?

Yes. Alongside the standard financial and operational reports, you can build custom reports, save them for your team and schedule them to be emailed on a regular basis.

Security & data

Is my data secure?

Access is controlled by roles and permissions, every change is recorded in an audit trail, and users can turn on two-factor authentication. Administrators can also require it for everyone in the organisation.

Who can see my business data?

Only the people you invite, and only within the modules and departments their role allows. Administrators can review and change access at any time.

Can I export my data?

Yes. Lists and reports can be exported to Excel, CSV or PDF at any time, so your data is always yours.

Support

How do I contact support?

Email support@wbizmanager.com or call us Monday to Friday, 8am to 6pm WAT. Email replies are sent within one business day.

Where can I find step-by-step guides?

The Help & Support centre has searchable how-to guides for every module, from setting up your organisation to running payroll.
