Who is iBroka for?

Licensed insurance brokerages in Nigeria, from one-person firms to teams with several branches.

What happens after the 30-day trial?

Choose a plan to keep going. Everything you set up during the trial stays in place.

Can I bring my existing spreadsheets?

Yes. Import your clients and policies, and we'll help with the first import.

Does iBroka help with the 2025 reform act?

iBroka tracks premium received and remittance due for every insurer and policy, so you can see your obligations and meet them on time.

Do I need servers or IT staff?

No. iBroka runs in the cloud on Microsoft Azure and works in any modern browser.

Can I add users later?

Yes. Move up a plan at any time; your data stays where it is.

Which classes of business are supported?

All of them, including motor, fire and special perils, marine, aviation, agric and life.
