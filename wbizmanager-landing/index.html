What is wBizManager?

wBizManager is a cloud-based business management and ERP solution that helps businesses manage sales, accounting, inventory, purchasing, customer management, HR, expenses and reporting.

Who is wBizManager for?

wBizManager is designed primarily for SMEs and growing businesses that need a more structured way to manage core operations.

Is wBizManager cloud-based?

Yes. wBizManager is delivered as a cloud-based business management solution.

Can I manage inventory?

Yes. The inventory module helps businesses track stock and connect inventory information with products, purchases, sales and invoices.

Can I manage accounting activities?

Yes. The platform includes accounting and bank reconciliation functionality, together with expense and reporting capabilities.

Can I manage employees?

Yes. The HR module supports employee information, administrative resources and performance appraisal.

Can I create invoices?

Yes. Businesses can create and send invoices online.

Can I track expenses?

Yes. Businesses can capture costs, manage expense claims and view spending.

Can I manage fixed assets?

Yes. Businesses can record fixed assets and manage depreciation and disposals.

Is there a free trial?

Yes. A free trial option is currently offered.

How do I get started?

Request a demo or start the available free trial. A member of the wBizManager team can also discuss your business requirements with you.
