What is iBroka?

iBroka is easy-to-manage, fully integrated software for insurance brokers. It manages clients, leads, policies, approvals, claims, insurers, premium collection, remittance to insurers, accounting, reporting and HR in one system.

Who is iBroka for?

Insurance brokerages of any size, from small teams placing motor and retail cover to firms handling large corporate, specialty and employee benefit accounts.

Can we track leads before they become clients?

Yes. Capture leads one by one or in bulk, follow them through quoting, and convert a lead into a client and a policy without retyping anything.

Does iBroka handle remittance to insurers?

Yes. Premium you receive is matched to the policy, your commission is worked out, and the balance owed to each insurer shows in the remittance schedule until you pay it.

Can it remind us about renewals?

Yes. Policies coming up for renewal are flagged ahead of their end date, and you can send renewal notices to clients from the policy itself.

Can we bring our existing book over?

Yes. Our team helps you import clients, open policies and opening balances, so you start on iBroka with your full book in place.

Where is our data kept?

iBroka runs on Microsoft Azure cloud services, with in-country experts available 24/7, every day of the year.
