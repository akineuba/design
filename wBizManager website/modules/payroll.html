Payroll· part of Human Resources

Pay everyone correctly, on time, every time.

Run payroll from one set of employee records, with statutory deductions worked out for you, payslips your staff can see themselves, and entries posted straight to Accounting.

Book a demo See every feature

Sound familiar?

What finance and payroll teams tell us before they move payroll to wBizManager.

Deductions worked out by hand

Allowances, loans and recoveries live in spreadsheets, so every run needs checking twice.

Statutory remittances are stressful

PAYE, pension and NHF have different rates, recipients and deadlines to track.

Payroll waits for everything else

Hours, overtime, leave and loans arrive from different people at the last minute.

01

Salary structures and pay grades

Keep each employee’s salary, components and pay grade in one place, and set benefits once so they apply every period.

  • Earnings marked as taxable or pensionable
  • Pay grades and bands for every role
  • Recurring benefits such as pension and health cover
02

Additions, deductions and loans

Add allowances, bonuses and other earnings, and apply deductions such as loan repayments and recoveries. Leave, loans and expenses feed the run automatically.

  • Recurring or one-off items, added singly or in bulk
  • Loan repayments deducted on schedule
  • Expense refunds and unpaid leave carried in
03

Statutory compliance and remittance

Statutory deductions are calculated with every run, and remittances to the authorities are tracked to the due date. Rent relief is captured and applied to PAYE.

  • PAYE, pension, NHF, NSITF and ITF
  • Due dates and receipts for every remittance
  • Rent relief applications checked and applied
04

Payslips your team can see themselves

Generate branded payslips from a template you control. Employees view their payslips, year-to-date totals and tax summary through self-service.

  • Your logo, layout and wording
  • Published to the app and the web
  • Download or email as PDF
05

Pay salaries and post to Accounting

Pay salaries through a bank payment integration, and post payroll and loan entries to your accounting records without re-keying them.

  • Payment batches by payroll group
  • Bank files for your corporate accounts
  • Journals posted to Accounting automatically

Does it handle statutory deductions?

Yes. PAYE, pension, NHF and other statutory deductions are calculated with every run, and remittances are tracked to their due dates.

Can employees see their own payslips?

Yes. Payslips are published to self-service in the wBizManager app and on the web.

Can I use Payroll without Attendance and Timesheets?

Yes. Payroll works on its own. If you also use Attendance and Timesheets, approved hours and overtime flow into the run automatically.

How are part-month starters and leavers paid?

Their pay is pro-rated for the days they worked in the period.

Does it support more than one company or branch?

Yes. Run payroll for several company entities and branches, each with its own groups and rules.
