Attendance & Timesheets· part of Human Resources

Know who is at work, and every hour they give.

Employees clock in from their phone, the web or a terminal at the door, and log their time against projects and tasks. Managers approve from one inbox, and HR stops chasing corrections.

Book a demo See every feature

Sound familiar?

What HR, managers and payroll teams tell us before they move attendance and timesheets to wBizManager.

Attendance on paper or in separate tools

Sign-in books and spreadsheets arrive late, and every record is open to dispute.

Timesheets late and incomplete

Managers chase missing hours at the end of every period, and payroll waits for them.

Overtime worked out by hand

Hours, approvals and rates are pieced together from messages, so mistakes follow.

01

Clock in from anywhere

Employees check in and out from the web, the wBizManager app or an attendance terminal, and start and end breaks under the rules of their policy.

  • Web, mobile and terminal check-in, with enrolment for terminals
  • Location checked against each site’s geofence
  • Consent is asked for, recorded and can be withdrawn
02

Policies, schedules and rosters

Set working hours, grace periods and lateness rules once, then assign them by person, department or branch. Build shifts and rosters, and employees acknowledge their schedule.

  • Policy versions kept over time, with a preview of who is affected
  • Day, evening and night shifts on one roster
  • Holiday work goes through approval
03

Exceptions, corrections and overrides

Missing check-outs and unusual entries are flagged for the manager. Employees request corrections, managers approve them, and HR can record an entry when needed.

  • One queue for every exception and request
  • Approvals can be delegated during absence
  • Every change kept in the audit trail
04

Timesheets, checked before they are sent

Employees log time by day or week against time types, projects and tasks. Rules catch gaps and errors before the timesheet goes for approval.

  • Weekly, bi-weekly or monthly periods and timesheet groups
  • Attach site logs and other supporting files
  • Automatic reminders for missing or late timesheets
05

Approve hours and overtime in one inbox

Managers review timesheets, overtime and corrections from one inbox, and approve or return them with a full audit trail. Rates turn approved hours into project costs.

  • Overtime counts only once it is approved
  • Hours costed against projects at their rates
  • Reports by person, team, project and period

From clock-in to approved hours.

Can staff clock in from their phones?

Yes. Employees can use the wBizManager app, the web or an attendance terminal at the door.

Is employee location or biometric data protected?

Employees see a notice and give consent before location or biometric data is used, and they can withdraw it at any time.

Can we have different rules for different sites or shifts?

Yes. Create attendance policies for each site, team or shift and assign them by person, department or branch.

Do I need Payroll to use Attendance and Timesheets?

No. It works on its own. If you add Payroll, approved hours and overtime flow into the pay run automatically.

Does it support more than one company or branch?

Yes. It supports branches and multiple company entities in the same group.
