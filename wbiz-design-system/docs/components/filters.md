# Filters: FilterDropdown and FilterBar

## FilterDropdown (list pages)
Trigger from the accounting toolbars ([Customers](https://www.figma.com/design/TJAupALP2AYuN7NXNlY33g/w-Biz?node-id=191-3274)):
borderless, 40px, three-line `FilterLinesIcon`, Inter 14 `fg`. Open or applied: `primary-subtle` fill and primary text, plus a count badge.
The panel follows the new features filter panel (7099:7787).

## FilterBar (report pages)
Figma: [filter, General ledger](https://www.figma.com/design/TJAupALP2AYuN7NXNlY33g/w-Biz?node-id=1752-21749), two variants.
White card, line-subtle border, 8px radius, 16px padding. Fields, "More Filters" and Apply are 161px wide (`size-filter-control`), fields 34px tall.
- **Default:** main field(s) · "More Filters" (white, primary border, `FilterEditIcon`) · Apply (primary).
- **Expanded:** "More Filters" fills with `primary-muted` and the icon turns primary; a divider and a second row of fields appear, ending with "Advanced" (tinted, primary text).
