## When to use
Attaching supporting documents to a record: purchase orders on invoices, receipts on expense claims, contracts on vendors.

## How it behaves
- Drop files on the zone or click **browse**; several at once is fine.
- Each file shows its name and size with a remove button until the record is saved.
- Files over the limit (10 MB by default) are refused with a message naming them.
- After the record is issued or locked, attachments are read-only.
