## When to use
The app's main navigation, on every screen. There is one menu for the whole platform.

## How it behaves
- Three levels: **module** (Accounting) › **page** (Fixed Assets) › **sub-page** (Asset Category). Any level can be a group.
- The current page is highlighted, and its module gets the blue bar. Its groups open automatically; other groups stay as the user left them.
- Clicking a group only opens or closes it; clicking a page navigates.
- The collapse button turns the menu into an icon rail; clicking a module icon in the rail expands the menu at that module.
- Long labels are cut with "…" and show in full on hover.

## Content
Add new screens to the module where users would look for them; avoid new top-level modules. Labels match page titles.
