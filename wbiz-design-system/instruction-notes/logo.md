## When to use
`full` in the sidebar header and sign-in screens; `mark` in the collapsed sidebar and small spaces. Don't recolour, stretch or put it on a busy background.

## Dark mode
Dark mode uses the white logo supplied by design (`wbizmanager-logo-dark.png`, and `wbizmanager-mark-dark.png` for the symbol). The component switches automatically; never invert or filter the light logo.
