## When to use
`full` in the sidebar header and sign-in screens; `mark` in the collapsed sidebar and small spaces. Don't recolour, stretch or put it on a busy background.

## Dark mode
There is no dark logo asset yet. In dark mode the component inverts the PNG and rotates its hues back so the navy wordmark reads on dark surfaces; this is the only recolouring allowed, and it is temporary until design supplies a dark version.
