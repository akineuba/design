## When to use
Showing a person: the first column of people tables, assignees, approvers, the signed-in user in the top bar.

## How it behaves
- Shows the photo when there is one, otherwise initials (first and last name).
- `blue` everywhere, including the top bar. `neutral` only for system or unknown users.
- In tables use `AvatarLabel` (avatar + name, optional email or job title underneath).
