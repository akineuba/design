## When to use
Switching between two to four views or modes of the same content: List / Card view, Inventory / Free Text lines.

## How it behaves
Switches instantly, without a save. The choice of view (List / Card) is remembered per user.

| Appearance | Use it when |
|---|---|
| `pill` | How a line or form is filled in. |
| `boxed` | View toggles in a toolbar. |
