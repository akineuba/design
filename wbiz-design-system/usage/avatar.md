## When to use
Showing a person: the first column of people tables (customers, employees, candidates), assignees, the signed-in user.

## Choosing a tone
| Tone | Use it when |
|---|---|
| `blue` | People in tables and lists. |
| `neutral` | The signed-in user in the top bar. |

Use `AvatarLabel` in tables to show the name (and an optional second line like email or job title) next to the avatar.
If a photo exists pass `src`; initials are the fallback.
