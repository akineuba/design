## When to use
Short decisions that block the page: move contacts to an audience, confirm a delete. Anything with many fields goes in a `Drawer`.

## How it behaves
Focus moves to the first field and is trapped; Esc and the close button dismiss it. `ConfirmDialog` is the destructive variant with a red action and a loading state.

## Placement
Centred. On mobile, prefer a bottom sheet.
