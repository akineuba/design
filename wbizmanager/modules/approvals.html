Approvals · part of wBizManager

Sign-off without the paper trail.

Move material requisitions, purchase orders and other requests through one clear approval workflow, with every decision recorded against the request it belongs to.

Get Started Talk to our team
01

One queue for everything awaiting sign-off

Approvers see every request waiting on them in a single list, with the details they need to decide, instead of chasing forms, emails and signatures.

02

Workflows that match how you work

Configure who approves which requests, so each one goes to the right people in the right order without anyone having to route it by hand.

03

A clear record of every decision

Each approval or rejection is stored with the request, so you can always see who signed off, when, and what happened next.
