Suppliers and purchases, in order.

Supplier records in one place

Keep supplier details, contacts and purchase history together, so anyone placing an order can see who you buy from and on what terms.

02

Purchase orders and quotations, tracked

Raise requests for quotation and purchase orders, send them for approval and follow each one against its supplier until the goods arrive.

03

Purchasing reports

See what you are buying, from whom and how it connects to stock levels and spending, so purchasing decisions are based on the whole picture.
