Getting started

How do I set up my organisation for the first time?

Go to Settings → Organisation and enter your company details, base currency, financial year and tax settings. The setup checklist on your dashboard walks you through the rest — chart of accounts, users and opening balances — and ticks each step off as you finish it.

How do I invite my team?

Open Settings → Users and choose Invite user. Enter their work email and pick a role such as Accountant, Storekeeper or HR Officer. They receive an email to set a password, and see only the modules and departments their role allows.

Can I import data from my old system or spreadsheets?

Yes. Customers, suppliers, items, opening balances and employees can all be imported from CSV or Excel. Use Bulk Upload on each list to download the template, fill it in and upload it — wBizManager checks every row and tells you exactly what to fix before anything is saved.

Accounting

How do I reconcile a bank account?

Go to Accounting → Bank Accounts, choose the account and select Reconcile. Upload your bank statement or enter the closing balance, then match each statement line to a recorded transaction. Unmatched lines can be turned into new entries in one click.

How do I close a financial period?

Once every reconciliation is done, open Accounting → Period Close and choose the month. wBizManager lists anything still open, such as unposted journals or unreconciled accounts. Closing the period locks it so no back-dated entries can change your reported figures.

Can I post journals in another currency?

Yes. Enable multi-currency under Settings → Currencies, then choose the currency when you create the journal. Exchange rates can be entered manually or updated daily, and realised gains or losses are posted automatically on settlement.

Sales & invoicing

How do I record a partial payment against an invoice?

Open Sales → Receive Payment, choose the customer and enter the amount received. Allocate it across one or more open invoices; any invoice not paid in full stays open with its remaining balance, and the customer statement updates straight away.

How do I issue a credit note?

Open the original invoice and choose Issue credit note, or go to Sales → Credit Notes → New. Select the lines being credited and a reason. Once posted, the credit is applied to the customer’s balance and can be used against future invoices or refunded.

Can I customise my invoice template?

Yes. Under Settings → Templates you can add your logo, colours, payment details and footer notes, and choose which columns appear. Every invoice, quote and credit note uses the template you set as default.

Purchases & approvals

How do I set up an approval workflow?

Go to Approvals → Workflows and choose what it applies to — purchase orders, payments, leave or expenses. Add steps, set an approver or role for each, and add conditions such as amount limits or departments. Requests follow the workflow automatically from then on.

Why is my purchase order still pending?

Open the purchase order and look at the approval trail at the top. It shows which step it is waiting on and who needs to act. Approvers are reminded automatically, and an administrator can reassign the step if someone is away.

How do I match a supplier bill to a purchase order?

When you record the bill, choose Match to PO and select the order. wBizManager compares quantities and prices with the goods received and flags any differences before the bill can be approved for payment.

Inventory

How do I transfer stock between warehouses?

Go to Inventory → Stock Movements → New Transfer. Choose the source and destination warehouses, add the items and quantities, and post it. Stock leaves the source straight away and arrives at the destination when the transfer is received.

How do reorder levels work?

Set a minimum quantity for each item per warehouse. When stock falls below it, the item appears on your Running low list and the storekeeper is notified, and you can raise a purchase requisition from the alert.

How do I run a stock count?

Open Inventory → Adjustments → New Count, pick the warehouse and print or download the count sheet. Enter the counted quantities and wBizManager shows the differences, which you can review and post as an adjustment.

Reports & dashboards

How do I schedule a report to be emailed?

Open the report, set your filters and choose Schedule. Pick how often it should run and who receives it. Recipients get a PDF or Excel copy on schedule, and only see data their role allows.

Can I build my own report?

Yes. Go to Reports → Report Library → New custom report. Choose a data source, pick columns, filters and grouping, and save it. Custom reports can be shared with your team, favourited and added to dashboards.

HR & payroll

How do employees request leave?

Employees choose Request leave from their dashboard, pick the leave type and dates, and submit. The request follows your leave approval workflow, and their balance updates as soon as it is approved.

How do I run an appraisal cycle?

Go to Human Resources → Appraisals → New cycle. Choose the review period, the employees included and the goals or competencies to rate. Employees complete a self review, managers add their ratings and HR signs off at the end.

Account & billing

How do I add more users to my plan?

An administrator can go to Settings → Subscription and increase the number of seats. The change takes effect immediately and the extra seats are charged pro rata on your next invoice.

How do I reset my password?

Choose Forgot password on the sign-in page and enter your work email. You will receive a link to set a new password. For security, the link expires after 30 minutes.

Can I turn on two-factor authentication?

Yes. Each user can enable it under My Profile → Security using an authenticator app. Administrators can also require it for everyone in the organisation under Settings → Security.
