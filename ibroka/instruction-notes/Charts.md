Area, bar (vertical or horizontal) and radar charts on the iBroka chart palette (UI rule 41).

- **Area**: change over time (premium per day).
- **Bar**: compare amounts across months or categories (cash flow, headcount). Prefer this to radar.
- **Radar**: only when the overall shape matters; exact values are hard to read.

Two or more series show a legend; one series needs none (the card title names it). Every chart has a Chart/Table toggle, so no value depends on colour or hover. Assign `color` per entity so filters never repaint series. One y-axis only.
