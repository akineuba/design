**MoneyDisplay** formats amounts: `full` (₦3,000,000.00) in tables and documents, `short` (₦3M) in summary cards and charts, with the full figure in the tooltip. Negatives are red; `signed` also makes positives green for balances. Right-align money in tables.

**MoneyInput** shows the currency symbol, accepts digits while typing and formats with separators on blur. Use it for sum insured, premium, excess and claim amounts.
