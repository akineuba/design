Right-hand panel for creating, viewing and editing a record over its list (UI rules 2, 3, 6). Sizes: `sm` 435px single-column, `md` 541px clients and contacts, `lg` 562px two-column forms such as policies.

- **Create/edit:** fields in the body, split into Accordion sections when long; footer Cancel then Save.
- **View:** read-only details with an **Edit** button that switches the same panel to edit mode.
- After saving: close, refresh the list, highlight the row and show a toast.
