Two ways to filter a list (UI rule 19):

- **QuickFilter** ("Status: All ▾"): applies immediately. Use at most two, beside the card title, for the filters people change most.
- **FilterDropdown** (Filter button): opens a panel of 34px fields; nothing changes until **Apply filters**. The button shows how many filters are active. Use for three or more filters, or ones that are expensive to apply.

Always pair with a Search field when the list can be searched.
